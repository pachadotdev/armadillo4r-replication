# Armadillo4r Replication

This repository contains my replication efforts of the following articles in the domain of Macroeconomics:

Baumeister, Christiane, and Gert Peersman. "The role of time‐varying price elasticities in accounting for volatility changes in the crude oil market." Journal of applied econometrics 28, no. 7 (2013): 1087-1109.

Caliendo, Lorenzo, and Fernando Parro. "Estimates of the Trade and Welfare Effects of NAFTA." The Review of Economic Studies 82, no. 1 (2015): 1-44.

Chari, Varadarajan V., Lawrence J. Christiano, and Patrick J. Kehoe. "Optimal fiscal policy in a business cycle model." Journal of polítical Economy 102, no. 4 (1994): 617-652.

Caravello, Tomás E., Alisdair McKay, and Christian K. Wolf. Evaluating Monetary Policy Counterfactuals:(When) Do We Need Structural Models?. No. w32988. National Bureau of Economic Research, 2024.

Each sub-directory has its own readme with instructions to install the packages and the accompanying replication codes.

BP13 and CMW26 include plots and presentations.

CKK94 consists only in CSV outputs.

CP15 has all the tables in the README.
